# What this study carries forward

The [two-card no-gap campaign closeout](../tower2-dual-qwen27-no-gap-2026-07-29/analysis/NO_GAP_CAMPAIGN_CLOSEOUT.md) is the most useful prior summary. Its [validation registry and status](../tower2-dual-qwen27-no-gap-2026-07-29/VALIDATION_STATUS.md) distinguish admissible replications from aborted, contaminated and non-steady runs. Some introductory README passages describe earlier intermediate counts; use the completed validated analyses for the final coefficients.

The earlier adjacent pair showed a strong position effect. Its informative automatic-fan 500/500 W population averaged about18.9 C hotter and428 MHz slower on the upper card, while that card requested about803 more RPM. That population closed at n=2 after strict closing-slope exclusions, so it is not an n=3 validation claim. The unchanged600/600 W pilot was retired after reaching its96 C cutoff; it is not a safe operating recommendation for the current four-card rig.

Two mechanism experiments established fan cooperation more directly. The [validated own-fan experiment](../tower2-dual-qwen27-no-gap-2026-07-29/analysis/FAN_ISOLATION_OWN_BOTTOM_VALIDATED.md) used three independently initialized, order-balanced blocks: raising the loaded bottom card's fan from30% to70% removed5.023 C locally and4.922 C from its idle upper neighbor. The50% point had the best observed clock/latency result, while70% supplied more thermal margin with a repeated latency penalty at the fixed board-power budget. Colder therefore did not automatically mean faster.

The [validated neighbor-isolation experiment](../tower2-dual-qwen27-no-gap-2026-07-29/analysis/FAN_ISOLATION_NEIGHBOR_LOADTOP_VALIDATED.md) held the loaded top at300 W,100% utilization and50% own fan while changing only the idle lower fan. Its three Latin-order blocks found that30% to70% lower-fan assistance reduced loaded-top mean temperature by4.984 C (paired95% interval4.655–5.313 C), increased mean graphics clock by15.090 MHz and shortened mean request duration by0.423 seconds. The lower board added3.911 W. That is measured board-power change, not a calibrated fan-only electrical curve.

The validated250,350 and400 W fixed-fan populations showed that moving a limited fan budget toward the lower card could improve the upper card's clocks and latency. The400 W B70T50-minus-B50T70 contrast improved top last-five-minute temperature by0.876 C and clock by22.874 MHz. The original400 W100-point budget had a safety failure and was replaced prospectively by the120-point matrix.300 W crossover blocks also exposed session heat and execution-order effects: an apparent thermal advantage did not reproduce even when clock redistribution did. Those distinctions are why this continuation records actual decode throughput, source versions and failed trials rather than ranking profiles by temperature alone.

The [spacing protocol](../tower2-dual-qwen27-no-gap-2026-07-29/SPACING_RESPONSE_PROTOCOL.md) proposed matched no-gap/gap/no-gap comparisons, measured gaps, calibrated ambient/inlet probes, card-position swaps and held-out interpolation checks. A protocol or stack-height forecast is not a measured spacing coefficient. The current four-card continuation has firmware-backed slot order but unmeasured shroud-gap/riser dimensions and inlet temperature; it does not complete that spacing experiment or validate an additive four-card thermal forecast.

This continuation physically tests four cards with one RAM-offloaded native DSV workload at275 W configured/enforced per card. The owner chose uniform fan commands and fan percentage as the within-rig relative noise proxy. That preference differs from the old asymmetric budget experiments, and percentage is not a portable airflow measurement. The prior evidence motivates coordinating the stack; this continuation's actual matched measurements and software-failure proofs support the practical installed choice. The unqualified formal comparison and omitted hot/reboot/soak checks remain explicit in [FAULT-EVIDENCE.md](FAULT-EVIDENCE.md).
